## Quick Reference

- **Inspiration:** Age of Empires II
- **Genre:** Turn-based text strategy
- **Setting:** Ghanaian civilizations, Gold Coast history
- **Engine:** Python 3, terminal-based
- **Civilizations:** Ashanti, Dagbon, Ga, Ewe, Mamprusi, Fante
- **Ages:** Settlement → Gold → Stool → Resistance
- **Resources:** Food, Wood, Gold, Kola
- **Victory:** Military, Gold Dominance, Cultural, Diplomatic
